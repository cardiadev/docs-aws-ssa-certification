## Overview

**AWS Direct Connect (DX)** is a dedicated physical connection between your on-premises network and AWS. Unlike a VPN that travels over the internet, Direct Connect uses a **direct connection** through the AWS backbone, offering higher throughput, lower latency and greater stability.

## Components

The Direct Connect architecture has three parts:

1. **On-premises network** — your data center with an edge router or firewall
2. **Direct Connect Location** — a regional data center where AWS has routers available for connection
3. **AWS Cloud** — your VPCs and AWS public services

### Connection Flow

```
On-premises → Customer Router (DX Location) → Cross Connect → AWS Router (DX Location) → AWS Backbone → VPC / Public services
```

At the DX location:
- **Customer Router** — your router installed at the DX location
- **AWS Direct Connect Router** — AWS-owned router
- **Cross Connect** — the physical connection you purchase between both routers

## Access Types

| Destination | Method |
|---|---|
| **Private resources** (private subnets) | Through a VPN Gateway connected to the AWS router at the DX location |
| **Public services** (S3, etc.) | Directly from the AWS router to AWS public services |

In both cases traffic travels through the **AWS backbone**, not the internet.

## Available Speeds

- **1 Gbps**
- **10 Gbps**
- **100 Gbps**

## Direct Connect vs VPN

| Feature | Direct Connect | VPN |
|---|---|---|
| **Medium** | Dedicated physical connection | Internet (IPsec tunnel) |
| **Latency** | Low and consistent | Variable |
| **Throughput** | Up to 100 Gbps | Up to 1.25 Gbps per tunnel |
| **Stability** | High | Depends on ISP |
| **Setup time** | Weeks/months | Minutes |
| **Cost** | Higher upfront cost | Lower cost |

## Pricing

- Charged per **port hours** used
- Charged for **egress data** out

<Callout type="warn">
**For the SAA-C03 exam:** Direct Connect is a physical connection (not over the internet) offering higher throughput and lower latency than a VPN. Charged per port hours + egress data. Supports access to both private resources and AWS public services through the AWS backbone.
</Callout>
